Investing in Puerto Vallarta real estate remains one of the most lucrative wealth-preservation moves for US and Canadian investors. However, once you take title to your beachfront condo, a critical financial decision arises: **should you operate as a high-turnover vacation rental (Short-Term) or secure a stable, fixed income through a traditional lease (Long-Term)?**

While traditional agencies often push a one-size-fits-all model, the truth is that your net returns depend on micro-location, HOA covenants, tax compliance, and operational efficiency.

Here is an objective, data-driven analysis to help you maximize your rental yield while preserving your asset's value.

## 1. Short-Term Vacation Rentals (STR): High Revenue Potential

Operating a short-term rental on platforms like Airbnb and Vrbo targets high-intent vacationers, remote workers, and winter snowbirds.

### The Financial Upside

- **Peak season surge:** During Puerto Vallarta's high season (November through April), daily rates in prime corridors like the Romantic Zone or Marina Vallarta double or triple.
- **Higher gross yields:** Successful STR properties can generate 10% to 14% gross rental yield annually when actively managed with dynamic pricing models.
- **Owner flexibility:** You reserve full access to your condo for personal vacations without negotiating with tenants.

### The Operational Reality

- **Seasonality fluctuations:** Revenue peaks during winter but dips during humid summer months.
- **Increased asset wear and tear:** High guest turnover requires continuous cleaning, linen replacement, and proactive maintenance against coastal salt spray damage.
- **Fragmented management fees:** Traditional local agencies often charge 18% to 25% hosting fees, plus separate charges for midnight repairs or guest concierge services.

## 2. Long-Term Rentals (LTR): Stability and Minimal Friction

Long-term rentals (typically 6- to 12-month lease agreements) cater to expats, local executives, and long-term digital nomads.

### The Financial Upside

- **Predictable cash flow:** Fixed monthly income eliminates low-season revenue dips.
- **Lower operating turnover:** Reduced cleaning costs, lower utility wear, and predictable budgeting.
- **Simplified management:** Less daily guest interaction and lower management commissions (typically 8% to 10%).

### The Operational Reality

- **Capped upside:** You miss out on premium holiday rates during peak Vallarta season (Thanksgiving, Christmas, and Easter).
- **Zero personal usage:** Your property remains occupied year-round, blocking personal stays.
- **Legal and tax compliance:** Managing leases under Mexican tenancy law requires strict tax compliance and legal vetting to prevent tenant disputes.

## 4. How Micro-Location Dictates Your Strategy

- **Romantic Zone & Amapas:** Best suited for **short-term rentals**. High pedestrian density, proximity to nightlife, and iconic beaches drive premium nightly rates year-round.
- **Marina Vallarta & North Hotel Zone:** Highly versatile. Excellent for **hybrid models** — operating as high-yield short-term rentals during winter and hosting mid-term snowbird leases during shoulder seasons.
- **Versalles & Fluvial Vallarta:** Ideal for **long-term rentals**. Growing expat demand and lower HOA fees create steady, hassle-free returns.
