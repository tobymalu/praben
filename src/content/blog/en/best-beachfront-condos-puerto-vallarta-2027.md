Picking a beachfront condo in Puerto Vallarta sounds simple — until you see just how many options there are. Between vibrant neighborhoods, quiet beaches, and luxury amenities, it's easy to get lost.

To save you hours of searching, we put together this list of the 10 standout beachfront condos and apartments for 2027. Whether you're planning a weekend getaway, a longer stay, or just looking for inspiration for your next investment, here's the best of the Vallarta coastline.

## How We Chose These (Our Selection Benchmark)

Following the criteria most valued by travelers and investors on platforms like TripAdvisor and Expedia, we filtered this list based on:

- **Genuine beachfront location:** Direct beach access without crossing an avenue.
- **Quality of facilities:** Infinity pools, gyms, 24/7 security, and impeccable upkeep.
- **Vibe and lifestyle:** Options for those who want nightlife and energy, and for those who want total privacy.

## The Top 10 Beachfront Condos This 2027

### 1. Sayan Beach / Sayan Tropical (Romantic Zone / Amapas)

**Style:** Modern luxury with a select crowd.

**The best part:** Its rooftop infinity pools and private restaurant with a direct view of the waves.

**Who it's for:** Couples or groups who want to be close to the Romantic Zone's best restaurants while still feeling a private sea breeze.

![Aerial view of Sayan Beach's infinity pool next to the beach, in Puerto Vallarta's Romantic Zone](/sayan-beach-vallarta.jpg)

![Ocean-view suite at Sayan Tropical in Puerto Vallarta](/sayan-tropical-suite.webp)

### 2. Indah (Conchas Chinas / Amapas)

**Style:** Contemporary architecture and open spaces.

**The best part:** Some of the finest finishes in the south side; you can literally hear the ocean from every balcony.

**Who it's for:** Discerning travelers after total privacy and postcard sunsets.

![Ocean-view balcony at the Indah condominium in Conchas Chinas, Puerto Vallarta](/indah-vallarta.jpg)

### 3. Molino de Agua (Romantic Zone)

**Style:** Tropical gardens and traditional luxury.

**The best part:** Its strategic location right where the Cuale River meets the sea. You can walk anywhere in 2 minutes.

**Who it's for:** Anyone who loves not needing a car, with the beach steps from the central pool.

![Tropical gardens and pool at Molino de Agua in Puerto Vallarta's Romantic Zone](/molino-de-agua-puerto-vallarta.jpg)

### 4. Icon Vallarta (North Hotel Zone)

**Style:** Cutting-edge design by YOO / Philippe Starck.

**The best part:** A private beach club, a resort-style amenities tower, and a long pool facing the bay.

**Who it's for:** Lovers of contemporary design, families, or digital nomads who want an active environment.

![Icon Vallarta tower next to its pool in Puerto Vallarta's North Hotel Zone](/icon-vallarta-condos.jpg)

### 5. Peninsula Puerto Vallarta (North Hotel Zone)

**Style:** Glass towers with minimalist elegance.

**The best part:** Wide wraparound balconies and one of the largest pools in the city.

**Who it's for:** Longer stays in spacious units with an unobstructed panoramic view of the cruise ships and the ocean.

![Glass towers of Peninsula Puerto Vallarta in the North Hotel Zone](/peninsula-puerto-vallarta.jpg)

### 6. Grand Venetian (Hotel Zone)

**Style:** Family-friendly residential, permanent-vacation feel.

**The best part:** Several heated pools, direct beach access, and proximity to malls like La Isla.

**Who it's for:** Families or investors looking for strong short-term rental returns.

![Common areas and pool at Grand Venetian in Puerto Vallarta's Hotel Zone](/grand-venetian-vallarta.jpg)

### 7. Paramount Bay (Amapas)

**Style:** Elevated views with a beach breeze.

**The best part:** Though it sits slightly up the hillside, it delivers the widest perspective of the entire Bay of Banderas.

**Who it's for:** Anyone who wants giant terraces for watching the sunset with a glass of wine.

![Panoramic view of the Bay of Banderas from a Paramount Bay terrace in Amapas](/paramount-bay-vallarta-condos.jpg)

### 8. Shangri-La (Marina Vallarta)

**Style:** Exclusivity and marina calm.

**The best part:** Huge grounds, a tennis court, a poolside restaurant, and direct access to the Marina beach.

**Who it's for:** Anyone after a relaxed setting near the golf course and the yacht marina boardwalk.

![Gardens and beach access at Shangri-La in Marina Vallarta](/shangri-la-marina-vallarta.jpg)

### 9. Tres Mares (Marina Vallarta)

**Style:** Timeless sophistication at the tip of the Marina.

**The best part:** A nearby boat launch and unobstructed views out to the Pacific.

**Who it's for:** Boaters, couples, and travelers who value the quiet of the city's north side.

![Infinity pool at Tres Mares overlooking the Pacific in Marina Vallarta](/tres-mares-condos-vallarta-marina.jpg)

### 10. Pier 57 (Romantic Zone)

**Style:** Modular spaces and LED lighting innovation.

**The best part:** Its famous rooftop bar with a 360° infinity-edge pool facing the iconic Los Muertos Pier.

**Who it's for:** Young professionals and couples after the epicenter of Vallarta's lifestyle and nightlife.

![Rooftop bar with infinity pool at Pier 57 facing the Los Muertos Pier in Puerto Vallarta](/pier-57-vallarta.jpg)

## Tips for Choosing Your Condo This 2027

**Looking for nightlife or rest?** The Romantic Zone has incredible energy day and night. If you'd rather hear nothing but the ocean at night, look toward the North Hotel Zone or Marina Vallarta.

**Check the tower's amenities:** Some offer at-cost restaurants exclusively for residents, which is a great perk.

**Maintenance and management:** If you own or are looking to invest, make sure you have a local property management team keeping the asset's value up — without you having to worry about it from a distance.
